The Takeaway

That last part is the whole point

What holds

  • Three reading controls, all persisted
  • An AI-assisted outline → draft → polish pipeline

What doesn't

  • Nothing publishes itself
  • No AI step can flip status to published
Outcome
The controls are three CSS variables and a bit of localStorage. The pipeline is three AI passes and one human decision that never moves.
