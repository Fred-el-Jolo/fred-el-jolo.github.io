    Hi, I’m Frédéric Guillaume

    I am a senior JavaScript developer currently working at Planisware in Sophia Antipolis, France.

    I have been working in web development for more than ten years and I am particularly fond of the Software Craftsmanship approach to coding.

    I am also the co-founder of the JSSophia user group, which aims to support the local JS community and promote JavaScript usage in the region.

    The name aesthetecoding.io comes from a punchline — Easy to learn, hard to master — translated to development: Easy to code, hard to master. This blog is my attempt at the thing that is easy to say, hard to write.

    My inspiration comes from facing new challenges, meeting people, attending events, and discovering new tools. Don’t hesitate to reach out.
